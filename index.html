About

I study and practice spiritual and embodied disciplines: Kundalini yoga, Sat Nam Rasayan, tai chi, qigong, family constellation, human design, tarot, astrology and syncretic divination. I work with plants and make herbal medicine. I trained in the three-year clinical program at Arborvitae School of Traditional Herbalism, I am the founding herbalist of Mugworts Free Herbal Clinic, and I have brought herbal medicine to O+ Festival, DWeb and Hackers On Planet Earth. Herbal Theurgy, a book, class and talk, joins plant medicine and ritual. I also write and compose. This site is an evolving record of that work.

Shown at Ars Electronica, Hackers On Planet Earth, DWeb, Recurse Center, Banff Centre, Visions2030, HERE Arts Center, Sphinx, NEW INC, New Museum, Yale CCAM, Black Sky (Crete).
