About

Art, infrastructures and herbal practice at the intersections of memory, networks, value, care and the more-than-human.

Eon Meridian is an artist, writer, technologist and herbalist working across computation, medicinal plants, ritual, sound, diagrams and resilient infrastructure. This site is an evolving record of works, collaborations, institutions and experiments.

Shown at Ars Electronica, Hackers On Planet Earth, DWeb, Recurse Center, Banff Centre, Visions2030, HERE Arts Center, Sphinx, NEW INC, New Museum.
