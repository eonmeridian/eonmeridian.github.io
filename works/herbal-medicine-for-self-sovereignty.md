Herbal medicine for the people who keep networks running, given at Hackers On Planet Earth in 2025 and 2026.

## Why there

The talk comes out of the practice of [Mugworts Free Herbal Clinic](../mugworts/), which exists to give people agency over their own bodies and health choices. Taking it to a hacker conference puts two literacies in the same room: knowing how to keep a network running, and knowing how to keep a body running. Both are infrastructure a person or a community can hold for itself.
