A piece on Constance Debré.

<h2>The piece</h2>
<p>It starts from <a href="../emu-butch/">Emu Butch</a>, the novel, which is full of references to
Debré. They were meant as scaffolding, to come down once the book stood up.</p>

<h2>The issue</h2>
<p>Animal Shelter is edited by Hedi El Kholti. Issue 6 circles class, sex, the archive and memorials,
with a large part given to poetry. Its writers include Dodie Bellamy, Chris Kraus, Eileen Myles,
Wayne Koestenbaum, Gary Indiana, Rachel Kushner and Colm Tóibín.</p>
